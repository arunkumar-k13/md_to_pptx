# AI-Powered Clinical Trial Intelligence

## Case Study: Oncology Protocol Extraction
* **Objective:** Automate structured metadata and endpoint extraction from complex clinical study protocols.
* **Data Source:** Over 120,000 multi-center oncology trial PDF protocols and FDA regulatory filings.
* **Work Carried:** Designed and fine-tuned a domain-specific BioBERT NER and relation extraction model deployed on Kubernetes.
* **Skills:** Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, FastAPI, Docker, Azure Cloud.
* **Insights Delivered:** Achieved 85% reduction in manual review cycle times and 99.2% entity extraction accuracy.

---

## Architectural Workflow
- **Data Ingestion:** Automated OCR parsing for multi-column medical documents and structured tables.
- **Semantic Processing:** BioBERT entity recognition mapped to MeSH and SNOMED-CT clinical ontologies.
- **Validation Engine:** Automated cross-reference verification against FDA protocol guidelines.
- **API Distribution:** High-throughput REST API serving real-time predictions to clinical research teams.
