# Zero Trust Architecture Transformation

## Slide 1: Executive Summary
*   **Objective:** Shift from perimeter-based security to a "Never Trust, Always Verify" model.
*   **Context:** Escalating sophisticated attacks and the shift to hybrid work necessitate a robust Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA).
*   **Goal:** Protect corporate assets, ensure regulatory compliance, and enable secure business agility.

---

## Slide 2: The Modern Threat Landscape
**Key Threat Vectors:**
*   **Credential Theft:** Exploitation of weak passwords and lack of MFA via phishing.
*   **Ransomware & Malware:** Lateral movement across flat networks following initial breach.
*   **Insider Threats:** Unauthorized data exfiltration by employees or compromised internal accounts.
*   **Supply Chain Vulnerabilities:** Third-party software and vendor access points.
*   **IoT/OT Vulnerabilities:** Unmanaged devices creating entry points into the corporate core.

---

## Slide 3: Core Architectural Pillars (NIST 800-207)
1.  **Identity Security:** Robust IAM with adaptive Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
2.  **Device Security:** Continuous health monitoring and management of all endpoints.
3.  **Network/Environment:** Micro-segmentation to limit lateral movement.
4.  **Application/Workload:** Secure access at the application layer, not just the network.
5.  **Data Security:** Data-centric protection via encryption and automated classification.
6.  **Visibility & Analytics:** Unified logging and AI-driven threat detection.

---

## Slide 5: Multi-Year Security Roadmap
### **Phase 1: Foundation (Year 1)**
*   Implement Universal MFA across all systems.
*   Inventory all assets (Devices, Users, Data).
*   Deploy Single Sign-On (SSO) and Centralized Identity Provider.

### **Phase 2: Optimization (Year 2)**
*   Introduce Micro-segmentation for critical data centers.
*   Implement Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR).
*   Begin transitioning from VPN to Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA).

### **Phase 3: Advanced (Year 3+)**
*   Automated incident response via SOAR.
*   Dynamic, risk-based access policies (Biometrics, Location, Behavior).
*   Full integration of Data Loss Prevention (DLP) across cloud and on-prem.

---

## Slide 6: Strategic Outcomes
*   **Reduced Risk:** Significant decrease in the "Blast Radius" of potential breaches.
*   **Improved Efficiency:** Streamlined user access and reduced IT overhead for password resets.
*   **Business Enablement:** Secure support for remote work and third-party collaboration.
*   **Regulatory Readiness:** Proactive alignment with global security standards.
